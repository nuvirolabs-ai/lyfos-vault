# Code of Conduct

Lyfos is built around trust. The same standard applies to this community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but be clear when something is unsafe or incorrect.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the work.
- Respect privacy. Do not post secrets, private vault data, personal records, or sensitive screenshots.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic comments or spam.
- Encouraging unsafe handling of credentials, personal data, or vault contents.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.

To report a conduct issue, email:

`hello@nuvirolabs.com`

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public issues.
