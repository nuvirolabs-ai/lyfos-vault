Bug Bounty

Lyfos welcomes responsible security reports. A paid bug bounty is not active right now.

What to report

Send reports that show a real impact on Lyfos users, vault data, authentication, release flows, billing controls, or public infrastructure. Include clear reproduction steps, affected URLs, expected impact, and any suggested fix.

Out of scope

  • Automated scanner output without a demonstrated exploit path
  • Social engineering, spam, denial-of-service, or physical attacks
  • Issues in third-party platforms unless the bug is in our integration
  • Missing headers or best-practice notes without practical impact

Recognition

Valid reports may be credited in the Hall of Fame unless the researcher opts out. No monetary reward is promised unless agreed in writing before testing.

Email hello@lyfos.in. Please follow the responsible disclosure policy.
