How your vault is protected

  • End-to-end encryption. Your vault is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM and a key derived from your passphrase using Argon2id (64 MiB, 3 iterations). The plaintext never leaves your device.
  • Zero-knowledge architecture. Our servers store only ciphertext. Even if our database were leaked tomorrow, the attacker would learn nothing about what's in your vault. We cannot read it. We cannot recover it. We cannot be compelled to produce it.
  • 3-of-5 release engine. To recover your vault for your nominee, three of your five chosen key holders must each independently release their share. Then a 14-day owner-protection hold begins, during which you receive alerts on up to four channels (email, SMS, WhatsApp, push). One tap on any alert aborts the release.
  • Open cryptographic primitives. Curve25519 (NaCl box), Argon2id (RFC 9106), Shamir's Secret Sharing — all standard, all reviewable.

What we cannot defend against

We owe you the truth: nothing is bulletproof. If your device is compromised (rooted phone, keylogged laptop), an attacker who has your passphrase can read your vault — because that's what your vault is for. If you lose both your passphrase and your 24-word recovery phrase, your vault is gone forever. We designed it that way on purpose: there is no master key we hold in reserve.
