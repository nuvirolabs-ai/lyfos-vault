Cancellation

Customers may cancel future renewals from the billing flow when self-serve billing is available, or by emailing hello@lyfos.in. Cancellation stops future renewal charges but does not automatically refund an active billing period.

Refund window

If a customer is charged in error, cannot access the product after payment, or was billed after a cancellation request, they can contact Nuviro Labs within 7 days of the charge. Eligible refunds are returned to the original payment method.

Non-refundable cases

Completed digital access periods, custom services already delivered, and misuse or policy violation cases may not be refundable unless required by law.

Processing time

Approved refunds are initiated within 5 to 7 business days. Bank, card, UPI, wallet, or payment-provider timelines may add additional processing time.
