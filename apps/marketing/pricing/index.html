Questions we get.

Why isn't there a forever-free release engine?

Because release depends on email delivery, verified key holders, owner-protection holds, and recovery alerts. Free Forever is for storing essentials; Vault pays for the release infrastructure.

Why one-time and not a subscription?

A vault is not a SaaS habit. Most people interact with it twice a year — once at tax time, once when something changes. A single payment for lifetime access fits that rhythm better than a recurring bill.

Is there a Family plan?

Not right now. Lyfos has two versions: Free Forever and Vault. Keep each person's vault separate for now.

Refunds?

Contact us within 7 days of purchase if something's wrong. No questions asked. Reply to any billing email or write to hello@lyfos.in.
