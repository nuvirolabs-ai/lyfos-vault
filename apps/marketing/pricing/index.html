Questions we get.

Why isn't there a forever-free release engine?

Because alerting four channels for 14 days costs money in messaging fees, and we want the engine to be reliable, not best-effort. ₹999/year covers about 65 SMS, 50 WhatsApp templates, and unlimited email — enough for several real recoveries with margin.

Why an annual price and not monthly?

A vault is not a SaaS habit. Most people interact with it twice a year — once at tax time, once when something changes. Annual billing fits the rhythm.

Do I need to buy Family for my parents?

No — they can stay on Vault. Family is for households where one card pays for everyone and you want it bundled. Each person still has their own zero-knowledge vault with their own passphrase.

Refunds?

Pro-rated for unused months. No questions asked. Reply to any billing email or write to help@lyfos.signorvale.com.
