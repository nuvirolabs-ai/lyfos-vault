The problem.

Most people die with a thousand small secrets nobody can find. Bank accounts forgotten. Insurance policies unclaimed. Crypto wallets locked forever. Family lawyers piecing things together for months. None of it has to be that way.

What Lyfos does differently.

01

Zero-knowledge by default

Your vault is encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know. We can't read it. Even if we wanted to, we can't.

02

3-of-5 release engine

Choose five people you trust. Three of them — together, not alone — can release your vault to your nominee when the time comes.

03

14-day owner-protection hold

If a release ever starts and you're still here, you get alerts on email, SMS, WhatsApp, and push. One tap aborts it. No questions asked.

04

A monthly balance sheet

Two minutes on the first of each month. Net worth, accounts, currencies. Your vault is a place you actually visit, not a folder you forget.

Our promises.

We will never see your vault.

The architecture makes it impossible. If we ever change that, the change requires a new app version you approve — not a server-side toggle.

We will never sell your data.

We have no data to sell. Your subscription pays for the service. That's the deal.

We will tell you when something goes wrong.

Full disclosure within 72 hours. Public post-mortems. Status page we keep honest.

If we disappear, your vault still works.

Lyfos is local-first. You always have a copy. Your recovery phrase decrypts your vault even if the company is gone.

Built around real life events.

The release engine isn't a feature. It's the whole point. We walked through it end-to-end before we wrote a line of UI.
