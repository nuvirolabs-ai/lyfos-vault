Secured

The most secure app you'll ever own.

Most apps promise they won't look inside. Lyfos is built so it can't.

Encrypted before it ever leaves your hands.

Argon2id + AES-256, on your device. The cloud only ever holds scrambled bytes — never anything we, or anyone, could read.

We hold no key.

No master password. No backdoor. No reset link. There is simply nothing on our side for us — or an attacker — to unlock.

Math, not trust.

Recovery takes 3 of 5 independent keys, split by Shamir's Secret Sharing. No single person — not even your nominee — can open it alone.

It works even if we're gone.

Local-first by design. Your 24-word recovery phrase opens your vault with or without Lyfos. You are never locked in, and never locked out.
