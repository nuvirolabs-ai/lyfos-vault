Why we built this

It was never about planning for the end. It's about making sure the people you love aren't left lost in the middle of the hardest week of their lives.

Most people leave behind a thousand small secrets no one can find — accounts forgotten, policies unclaimed, wallets locked forever. Lyfos turns that scatter into one calm, recoverable place.

The circle of trust

Five people you trust. Three to open the door.

No single person — not even your nominee, not even us — can open your vault alone. You choose five trusted key holders. Any three of them, together, can release it to your family when it's truly needed.

A release you control the whole way

If a recovery ever begins, you have fourteen days and one tap.

The release engine is the whole point of Lyfos — so we made it slow, loud, and reversible. Designed to protect you even in the rare case something starts by mistake.

Built thoughtfully

Small decisions, made carefully.

Zero-knowledge by default

Encrypted on your device with Argon2id + AES-256. We can't read your vault. Not as a policy — as a fact of the architecture.

A two-minute monthly ritual

On the first of the month, a calm nudge: update your numbers, watch your net worth line move. A vault you visit, not a folder you forget.

Practice the recovery

A safe dry-run walks you through exactly what your family would see — step by step — so the real day is never the first time.

Capture the mess

Paste a note, snap a document. Lyfos proposes a clean draft; you decide what becomes a record. No blank-page paralysis.

Unlock with your face

Face ID and Touch ID on mobile. Your passphrase derives the key locally — biometrics just make opening it effortless.

A 24-word lifeline

A standard recovery phrase opens your vault even if you forget your passphrase — and even if Lyfos, one day, no longer exists.

Our promises

The kind of company you'd trust with this.

We will never see your vault.

The architecture makes it impossible. If that ever changed, it would take a new app version you choose to install — never a silent server switch.

We have no data to sell.

Your subscription pays for the service. There is no second business model hiding behind it.

We'll tell you when something breaks.

Full disclosure within 72 hours and honest public post-mortems. Our status page stays truthful.

If we disappear, your vault still opens.

Lyfos is local-first. You always hold a copy, and your recovery phrase decrypts it — with or without us.

We tested it on ourselves first

We ran a full death-recovery, start to finish, before writing the UI.

The release engine isn't a marketing feature bolted on at the end — it's the reason Lyfos exists. We walked the entire path a grieving family would walk, and published what we found.
