One-paragraph pitch.

Lyfos is a zero-knowledge vault for the things your family will need after you're gone — passwords, IDs, banking, insurance, and the keys to all of it. Unlike a generic password manager, Lyfos is designed from day one for life recovery: a 3-of-5 release engine, a 14-day owner-protection hold, and multi-channel alerts mean your nominee can actually open the vault when they need to — and no one can while you don't want them to. Built in India, designed for the world, launching on iOS, Android, and the web.
