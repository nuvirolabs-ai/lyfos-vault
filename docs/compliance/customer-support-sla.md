# Customer Support SLA

_Last updated: 2026-05-28._

This SLA applies to the Lyfos Vault product. It is the public commitment we
make in addition to the privacy & terms pages.

## Channels

| Channel               | Purpose                                       | Address                            |
|-----------------------|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| In-app help           | First-line questions                          | Settings → Help                    |
| Email                 | Tickets, billing, account                     | help@lyfos.signorvale.com          |
| Security              | Vulnerabilities                               | security@lyfos.signorvale.com      |
| Grievances (DPDPA)    | Privacy + data rights                         | grievance@lyfos.signorvale.com     |
| Press                 | Media inquiries                               | press@lyfos.signorvale.com         |
| Founder direct        | Existential / nominee-in-distress             | founder@lyfos.signorvale.com       |

## Response targets (calendar hours)

| Severity / class                                     | First response | Resolution target |
|------------------------------------------------------|----------------|-------------------|
| **P0** — Nominee actively trying to release a vault  | 1 hour         | 4 hours           |
| **P0** — Owner unable to access their own vault      | 2 hours        | 8 hours           |
| **P0** — Service outage (status page red)            | 30 min         | per status page   |
| **P1** — Billing / refund / failed payment           | 1 business day | 5 business days   |
| **P1** — Account or auth issue                       | 1 business day | 3 business days   |
| **P2** — Feature requests, general questions         | 3 business days| best effort       |
| **P3** — Bug reports without active blocker          | 5 business days| next release      |
| Security disclosures                                 | Per `docs/security/responsible-disclosure.md` |  |
| DPDPA grievance                                       | 30 days max (Rule 6) | 30 days     |

## Hours

- Monday-Friday 09:00-19:00 IST: full support.
- Saturday 10:00-14:00 IST: P0 only.
- Sunday: P0 only.
- Public Indian holidays: P0 only.

P0 means an owner is locked out of their living vault OR a nominee is in an
active release window. Everything else can wait until the next business day.

## Escalation path

1. **L1** — Founder mailbox triage (24/7 for P0).
2. **L2** — Founder personal phone (P0 only, after L1 misses SLA).
3. **L3** — Status page incident + email blast (outage only).

Until headcount > 1, all tiers are the founder. Document time-zone of travel
in the status page if it would push response beyond SLA.

## What we do not promise

- 24/7 live chat (we are not staffed for this; not planned until 1,000 paid users).
- Phone support (email-only by design; phone is only for active P0 nominee distress).
- Recovery of vaults without the recovery phrase + passphrase (impossible — by design).
