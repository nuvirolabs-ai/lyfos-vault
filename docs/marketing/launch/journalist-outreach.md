## Follow-up cadence

| Day from initial email | Action                                                 |
|------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| 0                      | Send the personalised pitch                             |
| +2                     | Soft follow-up if no reply: "did this land?"            |
| +4                     | Final follow-up with offer to skip to written Q&A       |
| +6                     | If still no reply: drop with a one-liner thank-you      |

Aim for 3 of 8 to write a piece on or near launch day. If 4+ write,
that's a good launch press cycle. Press is amplification, not the
launch — the launch is the founding-member cohort flow.
