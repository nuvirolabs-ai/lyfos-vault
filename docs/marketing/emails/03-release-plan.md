A vault that only you can open is just a password manager. What makes
Lyfos different is the release plan — the part where you decide who can
get your records to your family when you can't do it yourself.

It takes about 15 minutes to set up:

  1. Pick five people you trust. Mix categories — a sibling, an old
     friend, your lawyer, your accountant, your spouse. Five non-
     overlapping circles is the goal.
  2. Pick one nominee — the person who will receive the bundle.
  3. We send each of the five an invite. They install Lyfos and accept.
  4. You finalise. Your vault key gets split into five sealed pieces,
     each only that holder can open.

After that, three of those five — together, not alone — can release
your vault to your nominee. With a 14-day owner-protection hold and
alerts on four channels for the whole window.

  → Set up your release plan: https://lyfos.signorvale.com

The release plan is on the Vault tab. If you're on a free account, it
unlocks when you upgrade to Vault (₹999/year). Annual, not monthly,
because this isn't a SaaS habit.
