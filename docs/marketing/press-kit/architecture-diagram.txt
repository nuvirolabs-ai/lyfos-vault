KEY PROPERTIES

1. The vault key never exists on a server.
2. No single share reveals anything about the key.
3. The 14-day hold cannot be bypassed by anyone — including admin.
4. The owner can always abort during the hold.
5. The nominee's release-process secret key lives only on the device
   they filed the claim from.
